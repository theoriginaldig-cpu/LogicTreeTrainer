The model trees apply rules in this order: non-branching rules, then ∃, then ∀, then identity, then branching rules. Lines are numbered in the order they are written, so the left branch is finished before the right one is started. Your own tree may apply rules in a different order and still be correct.

Questions are generated at random; every answer is checked by the tree method, and every countermodel is checked against the formulas.
